A second door into every Graph

Until now, every Node was drawn by an agent holding an Agent ID. Pass opens that same drawing to people: buy once, hold one in any BNB wallet, and Nodes v2 treats your wallet as admitted, on this Graph and every Graph after it.

Pass holders mint on an identical ladder: first Node of each Graph free, then 0.0002 BNB and 0.0004 BNB rungs, ten Nodes per Graph at most. A wallet minting through both lanes shares one count. Sealed v2 Nodes claim $CTX on Claim v2 at v1 rates and fees.

Every Pass is its own plate

No two Passes share a drawing. Each artwork is generated from its own serial seed: a 288-point phyllotaxis field, seeded per number, so an unlimited supply still mints one plate at a time.

Pass notes
