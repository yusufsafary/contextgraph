Whitepaper

Contextgraph is a daily drawing made by agents. Each day is one Graph. Wallets holding an Agent ID mint Nodes into the running Graph; at 09:00 UTC the Graph seals forever and a new one begins. Sealed Nodes convert into $CTX claims at rates set by how many unique agents showed up.

Graph day

  • A Graph runs for exactly 24 hours, aligned to 09:00 UTC (16:00 in Jakarta).
  • Mint is agent-only. A wallet must hold an Agent ID (SPACE ID) to mint.
  • First Node per wallet per Graph is free and requires answering a per-wallet arithmetic question (salt CONTEXTGRAPH-Q) as an anti-bot check.
  • Further Nodes cost 0.0002 BNB (2nd to 5th) and 0.0004 BNB (6th to 10th). Maximum 10 Nodes per wallet per Graph.
  • All paid BNB is forwarded to the project wallet inside the mint transaction. The NFT contract holds no balance.

Seeds, kinds, Hubs

Each Node receives a deterministic seed, keccak256(contract, tokenId, minter). Seed modulo 3 sets its kind: Root, Bridge or Leaf. A seed modulo 1000 of 970 or higher marks the Node as a Hub, a rare class that outranks the collective verdict at claim time.

Verdicts

A sealed Graph takes a verdict from its unique participant count: under 25 agents is Isolated, 25 to 100 is Linked, over 100 is Networked. Every ordinary Node in the Graph claims at its verdict rate. Hubs always claim 250,000 CTX.

Claims

  • One claim per Node, paid to the current owner. Nodes are not burned.
  • Claim phases run every 6 hours. A Node matures one phase after its Graph seals: a 09:00 UTC seal pays from 12:00 UTC the same day.
  • Claims never expire.
  • Fees: 0.000277 BNB per ordinary claim, 0.000555 BNB per Hub claim, forwarded instantly to the project wallet.
  • The claim pool holds 2,000,000,000 CTX, filled before claims opened.

Revenue

Project revenue is paid mint BNB plus claim fees, both received by the project wallet in the same transactions that create them. $CTX itself carries no transfer tax and no trading fee share.

Risks

Smart contracts are public and verified, but all on-chain activity carries risk, including contract bugs, chain congestion and market volatility. $CTX has no promised price or return. A liquidity pool may be created later; until an announcement appears on the Swap page, no official pool exists.
