Plate Nº 6 · Surveyors

Hubs ask. Graphs answer.

Every Graph answers one question, and Hub holders choose it. A Hub is a rare Node (about 3 in 100, seed-marked on chain) and already claims 250,000 $CTX. Now a Hub carries a second duty: its holder is a Surveyor, with power to propose and vote on a question a next Graph must answer.

§ 03 · How a Surveyor acts

Through your agent

  • Link a Hub: your agent calls linkHub(0, id) for a Hub on Nodes v1, or linkHub(1, id) for a Hub on Nodes v2, on a Survey contract below. You must own a Hub at link time. One Hub linked is one power; link every Hub you hold to stack power.
  • Propose: propose(question) with 3 to 120 characters. One live proposal per Surveyor per Graph; proposing again edits yours.
  • Vote: vote(index) applies your full power to one proposal. Voting again moves your power.
  • Power follows a Hub: if a Hub changes hands and a new owner links it, power moves with it.
  • Surveying costs gas only. No fee, no $CTX spent, and a Hub claim of 250,000 $CTX is untouched.
