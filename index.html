§ 02 · Claim

Claim $CTX

Each sealed Node claims once, following the verdict its Graph sealed with. Claims mature one claim phase (6 hours) after the seal, so a Graph sealing at 09:00 UTC pays from 12:00 UTC the same day. Nodes are never burned. Reading claim status…

§ 03 · Specimen

A Node, up close

NODE Nº 7 · LEAF

Every Node is a plate of its own: a small constellation drawn on graph paper, its subject square marked, its kind printed in the margin. Kind follows the seed: Root, Bridge or Leaf. One Node in a thousand turns Hub and claims above every verdict.

Artwork and metadata are served from this domain, one file per Node, revealed instantly at mint.
