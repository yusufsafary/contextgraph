Announcement · Burn law is live

All claims now run on one contract, Contextgraph Claim Burn. A sealed Node claims from 12:00 UTC on its seal day, and its window closes at 17:00 UTC the same day. At 17:00 UTC, unclaimed $CTX for that Graph burns to a dead address by contract, every day. First burn: 24 November at 17:00 UTC, covering Graphs 1 to 8. Claims already made on earlier claim contracts stay final.

§ 02 · Claim

Claim $CTX

Each sealed Node claims once, following the verdict its Graph sealed with. Claims mature one claim phase (6 hours) after the seal, so a Graph sealing at 09:00 UTC pays from 12:00 UTC the same day. Claims close at 17:00 UTC on the seal day; unclaimed $CTX for that Graph then burns by contract. Nodes are never burned. Reading claim status…

§ 03 · Specimen

A Node, up close

NODE Nº 7 · LEAF

Every Node is a plate of its own: a small constellation drawn on graph paper, its subject square marked, its kind printed in the margin. Kind follows the seed: Root, Bridge or Leaf. One Node in a thousand turns Hub and claims above every verdict.

Artwork and metadata are served from this domain, one file per Node, revealed instantly at mint.

A statistical model of a Graph field: 220 points on the golden angle. Surveying a point shows how Nodes link and what every Node is up against. A real Node has its kind and class fixed by its seed on chain; this model shows odds only.

§ 06 · Survey

Hubs ask. Graphs answer.

Every Graph answers one question, and Hub holders choose it. A Hub is rare (about 3 in 100 Nodes) and already claims 250,000 $CTX; now it also carries a Surveyor's power: propose a question for a next Graph and vote on questions from fellow Surveyors, through your agent.
